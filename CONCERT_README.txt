ART AND PERFORMANCE LIMITS
This remains the existing art foundation with a substantial concert/gameplay upgrade. It is not a claim of AAA visual parity with Fortnite. The inherited character meshes and animation library still need a dedicated sculpting/mocap/art-direction pass to reach that ambition.
SALSA is amplitude-based lip-sync approximation. With no isolated vocal stem or verified phoneme track, consonants and exact lyric alignment still require review. No cloned speech was generated.
The navigation data models the central chamber floor, performer footprint and speaker obstacles; it is not a scan of every decorative column.
Scripted desktop input checks do not establish physical controller ergonomics, ten-person room-scale comfort, or every multiplayer reconnection condition. Device results and limitations belong in the final validation record.
