4. Participants
At entry, each participant is assigned one Root Key. All ten keys are different. The Root Key sets the colour tint of that participant's view of the dungeon, their vantage point, and their appearance to the others: a figure surrounded by a glow in their colour. With fewer than ten participants, unassigned keys go dark (Open Item 10).
Group terms used in the sequence
Cantors: E (The Blade) and G (The Balanced).
Resonants: D (The Warrior) and C (The Foundation).
Harmonist: F (The Healer).
Whisper: B (The Shadow).
Seeker, Architect, Discordant, Ancient: C#, F#, A#/Bb, A respectively.
Character design notes (avatar assets)
The Founders (F#, A, Lord Lyrical): dark skin, broad features. Cymatics geometry appears on the skin when the Root Key activates and shifts with it. Wall carvings respond faintly to their presence.
The High Elves (E, G, F): luminescent. Glow intensifies on Root Key access. Steady, precise light.
The Stone Choir (D, C): compact, dense build. Earth-toned skin with faint vibrational striations on the forearms and collarbone. Low, dense glow.
Feral Choir, Pitch (B): wings folded under a cloak, hollow-boned build. Presence reads as a shimmer at the edge of perception; catches almost no light.
Feral Choir, Prowl (C#): feline, low to the ground.
Feral Choir, Howlers (A#/Bb): broad, wild build. Glow flickers unstably between crimson and black.
5. Lord Lyrical (11th Presence)
Control: none. Scripted character, present from 0:00 to 2:01, moving alongside the group.
People: Founder. Base asset: Jeru avatar assets.
Colour: continuous slow rotation through all 12 Root Key colours. Rotation accelerates at 0:59 (sphere pulse) and 1:21 (hand cycling, colour change every 0.5 s).
Root Key: none. Dissonant attacks have nothing to lock onto (1:16).
Actions: 0:21 spectrum ignition above the party. 0:34 counter call. 0:59 360 scan. 1:09 and 1:16 circling under fire. 1:21 Full Spectrum. 1:27 identifies the Discordant. 1:32 calls all ten up. 1:36 to 1:38 conducts The Word.
Spoken lines: 0:34 and 1:32 (Open Item 6).
6. Enemies
6.1 Sonic Orcs (Percussion Horde)
Root Key: D. Counter key: A.
Count: eight visible on emergence, then twelve, then more behind. Pack formation.
Build: massive, dense. Skin of overlapping dark resonance plates vibrating at a constant low frequency. Visible air disturbance around the body.
Weapon: double-headed tuning fork club. Each impact emits a shockwave that cracks stone.
Audio: subsonic grunts (felt, below hearing). Unison club strikes. Coordinated stomp producing a bass shockwave.
Movement: percussive, continuous advance. Halts only for the unison stomp (0:47).
Removal states: disintegration (0:39); seizure and drop (0:45); formation break and retreat into the dark region (0:50).
6.2 Sound Dragon (Frequency Predator)
Root Key: A#/Bb on the surface; E underneath, hidden. Counters: Anti-Frequency (Bb, the Discordant) opens the fracture; The Word (all ten keys) resolves it.
Scale: fills the chamber. Concealed from 0:21 to 1:03 as the chamber itself: wings folded against the walls, chest forming the ceiling.
Body: layered visible sound waves, rings of compressed air at scale. Scales shimmer and shift with internal frequency.
Eyes: two. Cycle amber, red, black without settling.
Wings: each beat emits a low-frequency shockwave that cracks walls and floor.
Breath: focused beam of dissonant frequency tuned to the target's Root Key. Narrow tracking beam (1:09). Wide arc sweep covering half the chamber (1:14).
Roar: felt first (chest, behind the eyes, base of the skull), then audible as a wall of dissonant frequency that cracks walls and splits the floor.
States in order: concealed; reveal; narrow beam; sweep; scream and adapt; seizure and fracture; exposed; unravel and dissipate into the Well.
7. Environments
E1. Frequency Vortex (0:00 to 0:20)
Spiralling tunnel. Cymatics geometry forms, rushes past, and dissolves in all 12 Root Key colours. Motion is forward and downward with spin. The other nine participants are visible in the tunnel with faint colour glows. At the bottom, the mouth of the Frequency Well: dark stone, carved cymatics, slow breathing motion.
E2. The Frequency Well, main chamber / Chamber of the Root (0:21 to 2:01)
Polished obsidian floor. Walls carved with cymatics patterns. Stone columns (three crack at 1:03). An unlit region ahead from which the Orcs emerge and into which they retreat. Identified as the Chamber of the Root at 0:59. Walls and ceiling are the concealed Dragon until 1:03; geometry after the Dragon detaches is unspecified (Open Item 7).
Floor states: intact (0:21); fissured along straight percussion lines (0:26); repaired (0:52); fractured by the wingspan (1:03); split further by the roar (1:03 to 1:09); covered in ten-colour cymatics art (1:38 to end).
Wall states: idle pulse (0:21); full activation for 2 s (0:59); slow wake (1:36); full bloom (1:38); art hold with single pulse (1:41); title assembly (1:51).

10. Open Items and Source Discrepancies
Slot 9 is named The Dissonant in the roster and The Discordant throughout the sequence. This plan uses Discordant.
Slot 1 (D, deep amber) and Slot 10 (A, deep amber-gold) are near-identical colours. Confirm distinct values for the colour code system.
Break audio (1:03 to 1:09) is described as an eerie ambient hold in the sequence and as six seconds of angelic choir in the production notes. Confirm.
Chord Barrage (1:21) is fired by the Cantors using two keys. The source does not name the keys. This plan assumes E and G.
Slot 4 (G) and Slot 10 (A) have no individual choreographed action. A is called as the Orc counter key at 0:34 but never executes; the Cantors take the front instead. Confirm whether to add beats or keep both as formation members until 1:32.
Three in-world spoken lines (Lord Lyrical at 0:34 and 1:32, the Seeker at 1:23) fall during the vocal track. Decide delivery: recorded VO mixed under the song, spatial text or glyph, or cut.
Chamber geometry after the Dragon detaches from the walls and ceiling at 1:03 is unspecified. Needs a storyboard frame.
The 0:59 two-second wall activation reveals something filling the chamber. Confirm what is shown: Dragon silhouette or abstract geometry.
Lord Lyrical's presence in the vortex (0:00 to 0:20) is not specified; the source describes him as always present. Confirm whether he appears in the descent.
With fewer than ten participants, unassigned keys go dark. Confirm behaviour of The Word (1:38) and the final art (1:41 onward) when keys are missing.
Sub-timings for the four Break beats (1:03 to 1:09) are not set. Set against the track.
