SONIC ORC — LORD LYRICAL
Rigged encounter asset, 3 October 2026

The existing Sonic Orc from your Meshy workspace has been downloaded, rigged in
Blender, finished with basalt/bronze armor and amber resonance channels, and
connected to the twelve-Orc encounter in the open LordLyrical Unity project.
The original horned armor silhouette is preserved. No replacement was generated.

RIG AND GEOMETRY
Character height: 2.9 metres, including horns.
53 body/finger bones plus two weapon socket bones; four optional hand/foot IK
controls in Blender. IK constraints start at zero influence so FK actions remain
editable. Enable the corresponding lowerarm/calf IK constraint to use its CTRL
bone. The source has a bent-arm bind pose; Unity uses a valid Generic Avatar to
preserve that pose and its authored animation. It is not a Humanoid-retargeted
avatar or a facial performance rig.
All vertices have skin weights; at most four influences per vertex.
Body LODs: 28,000 / 12,000 / 5,000 triangles.
Resonance channels: 1,472 additional triangles, sharing the same skeleton.
Two material slots per visible character. One 2048px base-color atlas;
Android import uses ASTC 6x6. Original source: 441,328 triangles.

ANIMATIONS
Orc_Idle: 3 seconds, looping.
Orc_Advance: 1.2 seconds, looping, in place.
Orc_Resonance_Strike: 2 seconds, one shot.
Orc_Stagger: 1 second, one shot.
Orc_Defeat: 2 seconds, held final pose.
Orc_Retreat: 0.8 seconds, looping, in place.
SonicOrcPerformance samples absolute time for synchronized show playback.
It also exposes independent clip sampling and resonance/dissolve controls.
The accompanying Animator controller provides the six states for normal use.

REMAINING POLISH
This is the first animated game asset pass. The movement is authored starter
animation, not motion capture. Foot planting, weapon grip and extreme armor
deformations can be polished further. Decimated topology preserves the source
silhouette but is not a hand-retopologized sculpt. The helmet has no facial rig.
The dissolve shader currently keeps the solid shadow/depth silhouette until the
actor disappears. Headset frame time, stereo rendering and Android device build
have not been profiled or validated; desktop checks do not establish PICO budgets.

This completes the existing Orc asset pass. The ten participant-role avatars are
separate from these encounter NPCs and remain the next character work.
