RIG AND MATERIALS
- Unity Humanoid avatars, body and articulated finger bones: 53 bones per source rig; Seeker has three additional tail bones.
- Four normalized skin influences per vertex maximum.
- Six starter clips per role: Idle, Walk, Ability, Stagger, Rise, Hands. The ability pose varies by role. Sixty clips total.
- Three BODY LODs at approximately 17,000 / 7,000 / 3,000 triangles. Hands and glyphs are additional; see Roster_Validation.json for exact totals. Hands now have separate distance LODs; first-person hands keep full detail.
- Separate hand meshes use only hand/finger weights and have closed wrist boundaries, so first-person placement cannot stretch vertices toward the hidden forearms.
- Separate cymatic geometry and glove materials support emission animation via MaterialPropertyBlock. Base skin, cloth, metal, trim and hair stay independently editable.
- Material remapping is attached to FBX source material identifiers to survive later mesh updates.
- Android texture import overrides use ASTC 6x6, maximum 1024 pixels; source textures are retained.

SHOW AND TRACKING
ParticipantAvatarPerformance samples clips at absolute times, holds the final Rise pose and supports nonchronological show seeks. Existing role positions, footstep/trail VFX and the music master clock remain in use. Shadow is hidden for its narrative interval, and all ten rise with the finale.
The local viewer sees their tracked hands and does not see a duplicate cinematic head/body. Remote bodies use head/controller tracking outside the show; during the show the authored role actors perform the fixed choreography. The physical XR origin and tracking targets are never moved by avatar choreography. Controller orientation offsets are exposed as leftGripEuler/rightGripEuler for hardware fitting.
Body/finger rig and synthetic hand tracking are verified in the Unity Editor. Real controllers and ten simultaneous headsets have NOT been tested in this pass.
