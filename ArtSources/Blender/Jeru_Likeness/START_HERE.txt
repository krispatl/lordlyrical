RIG AND COSTUME
69 deform bones plus eight IK target/pole controls. The body includes spine, neck, head, shoulders, arms, hands, every finger, legs, feet and toes. Twelve mantle bones animate four draped panels. Arm and leg IK/FK properties are on LordLyrical_Rig: 0 uses FK, 1 uses the named CTRL target and pole. Both arms and legs passed target-reach checks within 0.02 mm in the tested poses.
51 expression channels cover brows, lids, gaze, cheeks, nose, lips and jaw. Fifteen additional speech visemes are provided. Facial controls are rig custom properties; the head, mouth lining and beard carry corresponding blendshapes. Jaw, eyes and tongue also have bones. Separate teeth, tongue and a deforming oral cavity replace the photographed closed mouth surface. Unity jaw and eye bones retain authored animation rather than Humanoid facial retargeting.
Fedora and glasses are removable meshes. The tailored underlayer, dark mantle, violet lining, silver edging and cymatic seams use separate materials. The twelve-part clasp and seams can cycle colour through JeruPerformance.SetSpectrum without tinting the skin.
